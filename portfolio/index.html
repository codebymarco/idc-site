⭐ Featured Project

Uthando Primary School Complete Furniture Donation

📍 Umlazi, KwaZulu-Natal
📅 Completed: August 2024
🎓 Education

In partnership with the KZN Department of Education, we furnished 12 classrooms at Uthando Primary School with brand new desks, chairs, teacher tables, and storage units. This project impacted over 400 learners, providing them with proper learning environments. We used durable materials designed to withstand daily classroom use while maintaining safety standards for young learners.

The project included custom-designed storage solutions for teaching materials, allowing educators to organize resources effectively. All furniture was manufactured to SANS specifications and delivered on schedule, ensuring minimal disruption to the school term.

"The quality of workmanship exceeded our expectations. The desks are sturdy, beautifully finished, and our learners are so proud of their new classroom environment. Inkatha Development didn't just deliver furniture, they delivered hope and dignity to our school community."

— Mrs. Thandi Nkosi, Principal, Uthando Primary School
